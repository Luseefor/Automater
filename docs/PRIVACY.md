# Privacy policy

Tandem performs automation locally on your Mac. It does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, accounts, cloud synchronization, or network telemetry.

Macros and settings are stored locally in Application Support. Local diagnostic entries retain only a timestamp, broad error category, and generic message; they exclude clicked coordinates, typed content, window titles, and app names. Diagnostics are never uploaded automatically.

Clipboard history records copied text, images, and file references while Tandem is running and history is enabled. By default it keeps the latest 100 unpinned copies without an age limit. You can choose a copy limit and automatic expiry in Clipboard Settings or Options → Auto-Cleanup; both rules apply together, including while capture is paused and after relaunch. Up to 30 pinned items are kept separately and excluded from automatic cleanup. Removed history images are deleted from local storage; cleanup does not clear the current system clipboard. Items marked concealed, transient, or autogenerated by their source app are skipped. History is stored locally, is not encrypted by Tandem, and can be paused or deleted in Clipboard or Settings. Tandem cannot recover copies made before history was enabled.

Accessibility permission is used only to automate and inspect UI elements as requested by you. You can revoke it at any time in macOS Privacy & Security settings.
